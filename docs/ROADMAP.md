# Product Roadmap

The roadmap is outcome-based. Dates should be set only after pilot partners, hardware access, and technical capacity are confirmed.

## Phase 0 | Discovery and validation

**Outcome:** prove the problem is frequent, costly, and operationally actionable.

- Interview customers, market managers, sales representatives, and distributors.
- Observe the current shelf-check and replenishment process.
- Quantify baseline discovery, acknowledgement, and resolution times.
- Select 5-10 pilot products and 3-5 markets.
- Test QR reporting demand with a no-code flow.
- Collect consented product imagery across real lighting and layouts.

**Gate:** partners agree on the pilot workflow, target products, data handling, and success thresholds.

## Phase 1 | End-to-end MVP

**Outcome:** demonstrate a reliable closed loop from shelf gap to recorded action.

- Market onboarding and QR generation.
- Camera event ingestion and controlled product detection.
- Customer missing-product report.
- Signal correlation, prioritization, and alert routing.
- Incident queue, acknowledgement, and resolution.
- Analytics instrumentation and audit history.

**Gate:** staged tests meet minimum accuracy, latency, security, and usability criteria.

## Phase 2 | Live pilot

**Outcome:** prove measurable value in real operating conditions.

- Deploy in selected markets.
- Calibrate models and monitor device health.
- Run weekly operational reviews.
- Improve thresholds using false-positive and false-negative analysis.
- Validate customer reporting completion and report quality.
- Compare response time and recurring gaps with baseline.

**Gate:** pilot meets the exit criteria in [MVP Scope](MVP_SCOPE.md).

## Phase 3 | Trust and engagement

**Outcome:** increase signal quality and participation without adding harmful noise.

- Verified-report rewards.
- Fraud, duplicate, and anomaly controls.
- Customer status and optional restock notification.
- Expanded product-request flow.
- Better explainability for priority scores.

## Phase 4 | Operational scale

**Outcome:** support more markets and integrate with established business systems.

- Multi-region operations and role hierarchy.
- Integration with distributor, ERP, or order-management systems.
- Route-aware replenishment recommendations.
- Hardware fleet management and remote diagnostics.
- Service-level reporting and partner onboarding toolkit.

## Phase 5 | Strategic intelligence

**Outcome:** turn aggregated availability data into planning insights.

- Forecast recurring availability risk.
- Identify location-level assortment opportunities.
- Measure product-request conversion after restock.
- Add legally and operationally approved aggregate competitor context.
