# Product Vision

## Vision statement

Enable brands and retailers to know where products are missing, and act on that information, before shelf gaps become repeated lost sales.

## Product promise

For retail operators and consumer-goods teams that struggle with delayed visibility into shelf availability, Voice of the Market combines visual inventory monitoring with verified customer reports to generate fast, prioritized, location-specific replenishment signals.

## Strategic objective

Move product availability management from periodic checking to continuous, evidence-based action.

## Product principles

1. **Action over data volume** | every signal should help someone make a decision.
2. **Two signals are better than one** | combine camera observations and customer input where possible.
3. **No-app customer experience** | reporting must work from a browser after scanning a QR code.
4. **Trust before rewards** | reward only validated, useful reports.
5. **Human control for uncertain AI** | low-confidence detections go to review instead of triggering irreversible actions.
6. **Pilot before scale** | prove accuracy and operational value in a small number of markets first.

## Value by stakeholder

| Stakeholder | Current pain | Product value |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer | Travels to a store but cannot find a desired product | Fast reporting, product-request channel, and rewards |
| Market manager | Learns about gaps late or through complaints | Clear alerts and a simple acknowledgement workflow |
| Brand sales team | Limited real-time shelf visibility | Location-level availability and replenishment priorities |
| Distributor | Unclear urgency across stores | Ranked delivery needs instead of manual guesswork |
| Product/marketing team | Weak demand signal for unavailable products | City, store, product, and request-pattern analytics |

## Positioning

The product is not a full ERP or point-of-sale replacement. It is an **availability intelligence layer** that detects the gap between recorded inventory and what customers can actually find.

## Product thesis

If the business can combine reliable visual shelf signals with low-friction customer reports, then it can reduce the time between a product becoming unavailable and the responsible team taking action.

## Critical strategic choices

- Start with a limited set of visually distinct products and refrigerator layouts.
- Optimize first for alert precision, not maximum detection coverage.
- Keep customer reporting anonymous or minimally identified in the MVP.
- Measure time-to-action before claiming sales impact.
- Use rewards only after fraud controls and validation rules are proven.
